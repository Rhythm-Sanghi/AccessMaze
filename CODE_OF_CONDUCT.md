# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Critique work, not people. Welcome different experience
levels and accessibility needs. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted
sexual attention and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful contributions and limit participation to protect the project.
Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports should be handled confidentially
and fairly; maintainers involved in a report should not decide its resolution alone.
